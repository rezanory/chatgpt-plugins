# V5 no-new-training rationale

V5 performs no new model/representation selection and no new scientific fitting by design. V3 froze the internally selected protocol; V4 challenged it with raw-waveform representations under the pre-registered V5 policy and failed to displace it. Re-running selection after seeing V3/V4 outcomes would not add independent evidence and could create post-selection bias. V5 therefore locks the already-completed Kaggle evidence, claims, limitations, hashes, manuscript tables and reproducibility ledger.
